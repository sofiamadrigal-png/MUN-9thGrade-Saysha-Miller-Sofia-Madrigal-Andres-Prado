About Us

We are three students that want to create a website that will help people that are being bullied by giving them a space where they can feel safe and seen. Also it informs people what to do when they are being bullied or they know someone who is.

What to do if you or someone else is being bullied

Click on any strategy below to view step-by-step guidance:

Reach out to school counselors (like Maria Jose or Paula), a teacher, or your parents. Telling someone is not "snitching"—it is standing up for safety and well-being.
Take screenshots of hurtful messages, posts, or emails. Note down dates and times. Do not respond to cyberbullies directly; report the content instead.
Bullies often target isolated individuals. Walk with friends in hallways and during breaks. If you see someone being excluded or mistreated, invite them over to join your group.
